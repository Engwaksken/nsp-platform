# Founder guide: your startup on Hive NSP

Your startup has **one Hive profile**. You register it once, and your team, Hive programs and NSP services all use the same record.

## Register your startup
1. Sign in and open **My workspace › My startups › Register a startup**.
2. Fill in the company details. Only the registered name and the everyday name are required, and you can add the rest later.
3. If Hive finds a startup with the **same registration number or website**, you can't create a second profile. Choose **Ask to join** instead, and the existing team approves you.
4. If only the **name** matches, check whether it's yours. If it's a different company, tick the box to confirm and continue. Hive staff will double-check.

You become the startup's **owner**.

## Your team
Open the startup, then **Team**.
- **Invite** co-founders and staff by email. They must sign in with that same email address to accept. Links work for 7 days and only once.
- **Requests to join** appear at the top of the list. Approve them and choose a role, or decline.
- **What each role can do:**

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including managing owners and admins |
| Admin | Invite and manage founders, team members and viewers; edit the profile |
| Founder | Edit the profile and change data sharing |
| Team member, Viewer | See the profile (not money details unless you allow it) |

- A startup always keeps at least one owner. To leave as the only owner, make someone else an owner first.

## Several startups
If you're on more than one team, **My startups** lists them all. Choose **Work on this startup** to switch. You only ever see startups whose team you're on.

## Private details
Revenue, funding, traction and phone numbers are stored encrypted. For each group you choose who can see it:
- **Only owners, founders and admins** (the default for money)
- **Everyone on the team**
- **Our team and Hive staff**

Nothing in this section is ever public.

## Data sharing
Open **Data sharing**. Each choice is off until an owner or founder turns it on:
- **Prefill Hive program applications.** Your core profile is used to fill in IMS applications. No money details are shared.
- **List us in the NSP startup directory.** Your name, sector, location, description and website are shown publicly.
- **Anonymised ecosystem reports.** Your sector, team size and revenue band are included in combined statistics. You are never named.

Turning a choice off stops the sharing from that moment. The page keeps a history of who changed what.

## Change history
**Change history** shows every edit: who made it, when, and what changed. For private details it only records that they changed, never the values.

## If someone else saved first
If a teammate saves the profile while you're editing, your save is stopped so nothing is overwritten. Reload the page, then make your change again.
