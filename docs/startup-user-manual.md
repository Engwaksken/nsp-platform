# Founder guide: your startup on Hive NSP

Your startup has **one Hive profile**. You register it once, and your team, Hive programs and NSP services all use the same record.

## Register your startup
1. Sign in and open **My workspace › My startups › Register a startup**.
2. Fill in the company details. Only the registered name and the everyday name are required, and you can add the rest later.
3. If Hive finds a startup with the **same registration number or website**, you can't create a second profile. Choose **Ask to join** instead, and the existing team approves you.
4. If only the **name** matches, check whether it's yours. If it's a different company, tick the box to confirm and continue. Hive staff will double-check.

You become the startup's **owner**.

## Your team
Open the startup, then **Team**.
- **Invite** co-founders and staff by email. They must sign in with that same email address to accept. Links work for 7 days and only once.
- **Requests to join** appear at the top of the list. Approve them and choose a role, or decline.
- **What each role can do:**

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including managing owners and admins |
| Admin | Invite and manage founders, team members and viewers; edit the profile |
| Founder | Edit the profile and change data sharing |
| Team member, Viewer | See the profile (not money details unless you allow it) |

- A startup always keeps at least one owner. To leave as the only owner, make someone else an owner first.

## Several startups
If you're on more than one team, **My startups** lists them all. Choose **Work on this startup** to switch. You only ever see startups whose team you're on.

## Private details
Revenue, funding, traction and phone numbers are stored encrypted. For each group you choose who can see it:
- **Only owners, founders and admins** (the default for money)
- **Everyone on the team**
- **Our team and Hive staff**

Nothing in this section is ever public.

## Data sharing
Open **Data sharing**. Each choice is off until an owner or founder turns it on:
- **Prefill Hive program applications.** Your core profile is used to fill in IMS applications. No money details are shared.
- **List us in the NSP startup directory.** Your name, sector, location, description and website are shown publicly.
- **Anonymised ecosystem reports.** Your sector, team size and revenue band are included in combined statistics. You are never named.

Turning a choice off stops the sharing from that moment. The page keeps a history of who changed what.

## Change history
**Change history** shows every edit: who made it, when, and what changed. For private details it only records that they changed, never the values.

## If someone else saved first
If a teammate saves the profile while you're editing, your save is stopped so nothing is overwritten. Reload the page, then make your change again.

## Growth assessment and growth plan
Open your startup, then **Growth**.
- **The assessment:** owners, founders and admins answer about 18 questions on team, problem and solution, market, traction, finances and operations. You can save and finish later.
- **Your results:** you get a score out of 100, a stage, and a score for each area. These are a **self-assessment**: helpful for planning, not a verified rating. If Hive staff adjust your result, you see their reason next to your own result.
- **Where to focus next:** your weakest answers become suggestions, and each one says which answer it comes from. Choose **Add to growth plan** to track it.
- **Growth plan:** give each action an owner and a due date. Anyone on the team can update the status and add progress notes.
- **Reassessing:** reassess every few months to see your progress. Use **Printable report** to save a PDF for your board or funders.

## Opportunities
- **Opportunities** (top menu) lists open grants, accelerators, competitions, events and calls. Filter by type, sector, online, or closing soon.
- Open one to see **whether your startup qualifies**. Each rule shows ✓, ✗ or "unknown". Unknown means your profile is missing that detail. This is guidance only; the organiser decides.
- **Save and remind me:** we email you once, 3 days before the deadline.
- **Register or express interest:** where the organiser allows it, you can register for yourself or for your startup. The organiser sees your name, email and startup.
- **Recommended for your startup:** open your startup, then **Opportunities**. Each recommendation says why it is shown. Choose **Not relevant** to hide it.
- **Share an opportunity** you know about. Hive checks it before it appears.

## Funding and investors
NSP helps you meet investors that Hive has checked. It does not promise or arrange funding.
- Open your startup, then **Funding**. Write a **funding request**: how much, which types of funding, your pitch and how you will use the money. You can have one open request at a time; close it when you are done.
- Investors only find you when you have an open request **and** you turn on **Show our funding request to verified investors** under **Data sharing**. They see your name, sector, location, growth stage and the request. Nothing else.
- **Verified investors** shows who fits your request and why. Choose **Request introduction** to send a short message. Investors can also express interest in you; you decide whether to accept.
- Contact details are shared only when an introduction is accepted.
- **Data room:** upload your pitch deck, financials and other documents (PDF, Office files or images, up to 20 MB). Your team can always open them. To let an investor see them, connect first, then **Share data room** with an end date. You can remove access at any time. The **download log** shows who opened what and when.

## I am an investor
- Go to **My workspace › I am an investor** and fill in your investor profile. Hive checks it before you can see any startup.
- Once verified, your dashboard shows startups raising now that fit your focus (or **All**), introduction requests, your pipeline and data rooms shared with you.
- Your pipeline stages and notes are private to you.

## Experts and mentors
- Open your startup, then **Experts**. Everyone listed is checked by Hive. Filter by mentor or consultant and by expertise; each card says why the expert may suit you.
- Choose **Ask** to request a consultation or mentoring. The expert sees your startup’s name, sector and what you write. Hive can also assign you a mentor.
- Once the expert accepts you can see each other’s email. On the engagement page you plan **sessions** (online sessions need an https:// link), keep **shared notes**, keep **team notes** that only your team sees, and track **actions**.
- When you are done, mark it **completed** and give feedback. Your comment goes only to the expert and Hive; other founders only see an average rating once an expert has three reviews.

## I am an expert or mentor
- Go to **My workspace › I am an expert or mentor** and fill in your profile. Hive checks it before founders can find you.
- Your dashboard shows new requests, upcoming sessions and the startups you support. Your private session notes are visible only to you.
- NSP does not handle payments. If you charge a fee, agree it directly with the startup.

## Hiring (Talent)
- Open your startup, then **Talent**. **Post a vacancy** (job, internship, gig or volunteer role); it starts as a draft. **Publish** puts it on the public **Jobs** board with your startup’s name, sector and country.
- Each vacancy has its **applications**. Open one to read the profile and cover note, download the CV, move it through the stages, write team notes (never shown to the candidate) and propose interviews. When you change the stage the candidate gets an email; add a short message if you like.
- **Find candidates** shows people who chose to be found by startups. You see skills and preferences, not their email or CV. **Invite to apply** sends them your vacancy; applying is their choice.
- Only owners, founders and admins can see applications and CVs.

## Looking for work
- **Jobs** in the top menu lists open roles. Create your **candidate profile** once (headline, skills, CV) and apply with one click and an optional note.
- Your profile is hidden unless you choose **Startups on NSP can find my profile**. Even then, startups never see your email or CV until you apply.
- **My applications** shows each stage, messages and interview requests. Confirm an interview or ask for another time there. You can **withdraw** an application; its CV copy is deleted at once.
- You can delete your profile and CV at any time.

## Marketplace and startup directory
- To appear in the public **Startups** directory, turn on **List us in the NSP startup directory** under **Data sharing**. Only your name, sector, location, description and website are shown.
- Open your startup, then **Marketplace**, to post an **offer** (something you sell), a **request to buy** (something you need) or a **partnership** request. It starts as a draft; **Publish** puts it in the public marketplace.
- People message you on NSP. Owners, founders and admins reply; other team members can read. You get an email when there is a new message, without its text.
- To contact another startup, open its listing and **Send message**, for yourself or on behalf of your startup. Your conversations are under **My workspace › My messages**.
- If something goes wrong, use **Report a problem** in the conversation. Hive staff can then read it to help. Hive does not take part in sales or hold payments.

## Notifications
- **Notifications** in the top menu shows updates from every part of NSP; the number is how many you have not read.
- **Choose what you receive** lets you switch email and on-NSP notices on or off for each kind of update. News from Hive is not emailed unless you turn it on. Security emails (password resets and sign-in codes) are always sent.
- Every update email has a link to stop that kind of email with one click.
